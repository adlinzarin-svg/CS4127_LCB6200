Travel Information

Getting Here

Brunei can be reached by air through Brunei International Airport. Visitors can find flights connecting Bandar Seri Begawan with destinations across Asia and beyond.

Explore Royal Brunei Airlines

Getting Around

Visitors can explore Brunei using taxis, buses and water taxis. Water taxis are especially useful for travelling between Bandar Seri Begawan and Kampong Ayer.

Visa Information

Entry requirements depend on your nationality. Check the latest visa and immigration information before travelling to Brunei.

Brunei Immigration Department

Accommodation

Visitors can choose from hotels, serviced apartments and guesthouses. Staying near Bandar Seri Begawan provides convenient access to many attractions.

Best Time to Visit

Brunei has a tropical climate throughout the year. Visitors should prepare for warm temperatures, humidity and occasional rainfall when planning outdoor activities.
