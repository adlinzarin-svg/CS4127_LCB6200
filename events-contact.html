Events & Celebrations

Discover the celebrations and traditions that bring Brunei's communities together.

Brunei National Day

Celebrated on 23 February, National Day marks Brunei's independence. Experience the celebrations, community spirit and national pride.

Hari Raya Aidilfitri

Experience the festive atmosphere of Hari Raya, when families and communities gather to celebrate, share traditional food and welcome visitors.

His Majesty's Birthday

Celebrated on 15 July, His Majesty the Sultan's birthday is marked by national celebrations, ceremonies and activities across Brunei.
